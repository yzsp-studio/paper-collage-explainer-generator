## 🇬🇧 English

Turns copy, a story theme or an abstract viewpoint into a halftone paper-collage explainer animation, well suited to science communication, opinion pieces and social B-roll. The flow parses the input into a production plan and passes Gate 1 for confirmation before any generation, then locks still frames before rendering clips. Output carries tactile sound effects and follows unified global style rules. It is not aimed at live-action ads or complex typographic layouts.

### ✨ What it does

- Copy, story theme or abstract idea → halftone paper-collage explainer animation
- Gate 1 confirms the production plan before anything is generated
- Still frames are approved first, then tactile clips are rendered
- Audio strategy: tactile sound effects matched to the paper-collage visuals
- Global style rules keep the paper-collage texture consistent throughout
- Suited to science communication, opinion pieces and social B-roll
